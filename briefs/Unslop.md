# Unslop

Punctuation and headings give a draft away before the words do. The em dash
sprinkled through every paragraph, the Title Case On Every Heading, the nouns
that name nothing.

Write headings the way you write sentences, and make every noun point at
something a reader could count.

## Rules

`vale` checks every line of this on each draft. Treat it as priming rather than
a checklist to apply from memory, and expect the exact span back when you miss.

- **Headings** — sentence case. Capitalize the first word and the proper nouns,
  nothing else. Vale decides case by proportion, so declare your product and
  tool names in a vocabulary or a short heading full of them will trip.
- **Punctuation** — no em dash (—), no en dash (–), no ASCII stand-in (--).
  A comma, a colon, or two sentences will do the same work.
- **Vague** — name the thing. Not "a number of", not "various", not "several",
  not "certain aspects", not "key elements", not "stuff", not "things", not
  "solutions". Write the count, or write what the things are.
